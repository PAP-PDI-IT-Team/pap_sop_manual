AC Local: Job Order to live deployment

  1. Receive the Job Order: record the request, department, business need, and requester.
  2. Agree on scope: define deliverables, exclusions, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and proposed schedule.
  3. Review and approve: have the designated IT and department owners confirm scope before work begins.
  4. Build and validate: implement the approved work and record test results.
  5. Complete user acceptance: collect the requesting department's feedback and acceptance evidence.
  6. Prepare deployment: confirm authorization, release steps, rollback steps, and support ownership.
  7. Deploy and hand over: perform the authorized release, check the live site, and record the outcome and handover.

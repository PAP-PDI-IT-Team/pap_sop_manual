# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Treat interns, mentors, IT staff, and department representatives with respect. Welcome questions, provide constructive feedback, acknowledge contributions, and use language appropriate for a school and workplace environment.

Respect privacy. Use sanitized examples and share internal information only through approved channels. Follow school and organization policies when participating in this project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, threats, sexual content or attention, exposing private information, and retaliation against someone who raises a concern are not acceptable.

## Reporting and response

Report concerns privately to your assigned IT mentor, school coordinator, or organization HR contact through an existing approved channel. Do not post personal or incident details in public issues. A project-specific reporting contact is pending assignment.

Responsible school or organization representatives should review reports discreetly and fairly, protect privacy as far as possible, and determine appropriate action under their policies. Actions may include a request to stop, removal of inappropriate content, or restriction of project participation.

This code applies to repository contributions, reviews, project discussions, and related collaboration.
